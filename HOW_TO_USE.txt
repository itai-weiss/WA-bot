WA-bot: simple setup and use
============================

Before starting
---------------
1. Install Google Chrome.
2. Install Node.js 20 LTS from:
   https://nodejs.org/

First-time setup
----------------
1. Double-click install.bat.
2. Wait until it says Done.
3. Double-click start.bat.
4. A browser should open at:
   http://127.0.0.1:3000

First WhatsApp connection
-------------------------
1. The page may show a QR code.
2. Open WhatsApp on your phone.
3. Go to Settings -> Linked devices -> Link a device.
4. Scan the QR code.
5. After it connects, the timing page appears.

Creating a scheduled message
----------------------------
1. Recipients are always meta-groups (a saved list of groups).
   Click "Create meta-group", give it a name and tick its groups.
   A meta-group can contain just one group.
2. Click one or more meta-groups to send to them.
3. Write a message and/or attach files.
   Click "Add message" to send several messages in a row.
4. Choose a future date and time.
5. Click "Save timing".

Important
---------
- Keep the start.bat window open while messages need to be sent.
- The PC stays awake by itself until pending messages are sent.
  The screen can turn off, but do not close the laptop lid or shut down.
- If the PC was off at send time, messages go out when the app is
  running again, up to 6 hours late. Older ones are marked as not sent.
- Your phone can be off; it does not need to be connected.
- Keep the phone with WhatsApp online.
- This uses your personal WhatsApp Web session.
- Do not use it for spam or commercial bulk sending.
- The app only runs on this computer at 127.0.0.1.

Stopping
--------
Close the start.bat window, or press Ctrl+C in that window.

Local data
----------
These are created automatically on this computer:
- .env
- .wwebjs_auth/
- .wwebjs_cache/
- timings.json
- metagroups.json
- timing-attachments/

Do not share those files after logging in, because they may contain local session or message data.
